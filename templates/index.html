Bringing AI education to older adults

Technology should never leave anyone behind. Sage is more than a texting tool — it's the start of a broader effort to bring hands-on AI education directly to older adults in the places they already are.

🏡

In-home sessions

We're launching free one-on-one AI literacy visits to assisted living facilities and retirement communities — patient, jargon-free, and tailored to each person.

📚

Library workshops

Partnering with public libraries to offer group workshops where older adults can learn what AI is, what it can do, and how to use it safely.

🔒

Staying safe online

AI-powered scams are on the rise. Our sessions teach older adults how to spot fraud, protect their information, and navigate the internet with confidence.
